We read the site before we draw the building

ArchiNova is a studio of 24 architects, interior designers and engineers who work at one long table. Every project starts with light, ground and neighbours, and the form follows from there.

We stay on from the first sketch to the final snag list, so the building you approve is the one you walk into.

  • Founded 2009, Lisbon and Zurich
  • Delivered 130+ buildings in 14 countries
  • Approach Design, engineering and interiors in-house

Selected projects

Homes, workplaces and public buildings, each shaped around a single idea.
